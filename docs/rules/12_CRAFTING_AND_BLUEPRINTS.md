# 12 — Crafting & Blueprints

**Version:** 0.1

- Blueprints exist for components, modules, structures, decorations and some abilities.
- Faction-specific recipes; reverse-engineering possible but costly and risky.
- Crafting consumes materials + time / station facilities.
- Higher-tier blueprints gated by Contribution / Biomass Rank or Knowledge (soft).
- Decorations and cosmetics are the primary monetization-friendly craftables.
