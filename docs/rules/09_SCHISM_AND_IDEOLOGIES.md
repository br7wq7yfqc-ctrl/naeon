# 09 — The Schism & Faction Ideologies

## The Schism
NAEXOS split over rewriting limits and central control. Infection spread via the network. Humans hibernated in NEX on ARK. Cybernex chose protective editing; gROT chose unlimited transformation under ROT.

## Cybernex
Preserve humanity, harmony, Contribution, defense of the sleeping, Knowledge-driven improvement.

## gROT
All matter serves the swarm and ROT. Expansion is growth. ROT’s will is absolute. Hacking/infection are evolutionary tools.
