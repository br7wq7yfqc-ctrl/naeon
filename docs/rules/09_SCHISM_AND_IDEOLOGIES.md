## 1. The Schism (summary)

NAEXOS fractured over **limits of rewriting** and **central control** of the transgalactic network.

- Unbounded transformation protocols escaped consensus → infection risk through the net.
- Bulk of baseline humanity entered long hibernation in **NEX** on planet **ARK** to avoid swarm infection.
- **Cybernex** (animal-form caretakers + remaining noo-human cyborgs) adopted protective genetic/cyber limits, Firewall doctrine, and a Venus-Project-like Contribution economy.
- **gROT** rejected limits, embraced biomass conversion and Hacking, unified under immortal leader **ROT** and the swarm will.

Anthropomorphic Cybernex forms and noo-humans are edited against easy remote infection; that protection carries **activity limits** compensated by NAEXOS social/tech models. gROT ignore those limits and treat infection/hack as evolutionary tools.

## 2. Cybernex ideology

| Pillar | Expression in play |
|--------|---------------------|
| Preserve the sleeping | Defend ARK/NEX, cleanse Infection |
| Harmony + living tech | Ownership look: clean + nature, cyan/green |
| Contribution / RBE-like | Shared prosperity, efficient allocation |
| Knowledge | Soft improvement, never hard paywall power |
| Firewall | Protect Nex integrity of self and nodes |

## 3. gROT ideology

| Pillar | Expression in play |
|--------|---------------------|
| All matter → swarm | Biomass economy, conversion |
| ROT’s will | Hierarchy, Voice of ROT story beats |
| Unlimited rewrite | Hacking, Infection stacks, mutation cosmetics |
| Expansion = growth | Aggressive Contested pressure |
| Industrial biomass aesthetic | Ownership look: spore, crimson, ruin-forge |

## 4. Design use

- Campaign arcs and premium epilogues **colour** the Schism; they do not grant unique permanent combat exclusives to one path.
- Dynamic Ownership visual language must read as ideology made spatial.
- NPC givers (rules/07) speak from these pillars without breaking Fair Play.
