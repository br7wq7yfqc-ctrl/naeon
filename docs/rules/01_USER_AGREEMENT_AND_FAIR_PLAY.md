## 2. Fair Play Policy

### 2.1 Core Principle
**Skill, knowledge, cooperation and time investment should determine success.**  
Real-money purchases must never grant direct combat, strategic or economic power that cannot be obtained through play.

### 2.2 Allowed
- Cosmetic items, visual styles, emotes, trail effects
- Convenience features (extra inventory, faster travel cooldowns within limits, additional character slots)
- Subscription benefits listed in the Monetization Policy
- Educational tools and Prompt Studio token packs (content generation, not power)

### 2.3 Strictly Forbidden (RMT & Power)
- Selling or buying accounts, items, currency or boosts for real money outside official channels
- Any purchase that increases damage, health, resource yield, claim strength, fleet power, ability power or similar metrics beyond what free players can achieve
- Bots, macros, automation that replaces player skill in combat or strategy
- Exploiting bugs for advantage (must be reported)

### 2.4 Combat & Strategy Integrity
- Hacking and Nex-Firewall abilities are designed as counterplay systems, not pay-to-win tools
- Arena (MOBA) influence on the persistent world is deliberately soft and capped
- Knowledge Rank and educational mastery grant informational / QoL advantages only

### 2.5 Reporting & Enforcement
Players can report violations. We investigate and may apply:
- Warning
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent ban
- Removal of illegitimately obtained items

### 2.6 Soft Progression Philosophy (Design Contract)
- Cybernex: Contribution Score & Knowledge Rank
- gROT: Biomass Rank & Swarm integration
Both progressions are earned through play. Paid features may accelerate convenience or unlock narrative/cosmetic content, never the power curve itself.
