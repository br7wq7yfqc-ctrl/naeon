# NAEON — Voice Channel Achievement Thresholds

**Version:** 0.1  
**Depends on:** `11_ALLIANCE_AND_SOCIAL.md`, Fair Play / subscription rules  
**Constraint:** Voice is convenience/social — not combat power

---

## 1. Access paths (either is enough)

| Path | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Subscription** | Active Premium (or equivalent) |
| **Achievement** | Meet any one threshold below |

---

## 2. Achievement thresholds (starting)

| ID | Name | Threshold | Notes |
|----|------|-----------|-------|
| VOC-01 | Proven Operative | 50 standard quests completed | Either faction |
| VOC-02 | Claim Veteran | 10 successful claim flip participations | |
| VOC-03 | Lattice Guardian / Vein Raider | 100 Firewall cleanses **or** 100 successful Infect Links | Faction-flavoured same bar |
| VOC-04 | Alliance Pillar | 14 days in same alliance + rank ≥ Officer | |
| VOC-05 | Arena Signal | 25 linked Clash matches completed | Not win-only |

Meeting a threshold unlocks **alliance voice join** for that account; does not grant damage/HP.

---

## 3. Channel rules

- Alliance voice: members with access; moderation rank 3–4.
- No global free shout voice in open world at launch (spam/risk).
- NPC voice playback (TTS) may be free for main campaign lines; unrestricted free-form NPC voice stays quota/sub gated if cost-heavy.

---

## 4. Revocation

Fair Play bans may remove voice. Subscription lapse removes sub path but **keeps** achievement path if earned.
