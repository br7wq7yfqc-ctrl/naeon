# 04 — Global Balance Framework

**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-05

## 1. Guiding Principles

1. **No hard Pay-to-Win** — real money never buys permanent combat power that cannot be earned.
2. **Asymmetry with parity** — Cybernex and gROT feel different but both can win any major mode.
3. **Soft > Hard power** for Knowledge, subscription and educational benefits.
4. **Counterplay first** — every strong tool has a visible answer.
5. **Time-to-impact** must stay fun in TPS, Space and Strategy layers.
6. **Economic sinks and sources** must be controllable to prevent runaway inflation.

## 2. Power Axes

We track balance across these axes:

- Burst vs Sustained damage
- Control (CC) vs Mobility
- Information vs Fog-of-war
- Early-game vs Late-game strength
- Solo power vs Group/Alliance power
- Temporary (Arena) vs Persistent (MMO map) influence

## 3. Soft Caps & Diminishing Returns

- Infection stacks: soft cap, then strongly diminishing returns.
- Claim strength from Arena: hard monthly/seasonal cap.
- Knowledge combat benefits: strictly informational / QoL, never raw multipliers.
- Contribution / Biomass Rank: diminishing returns on the highest tiers for direct combat power.

## 4. Layer-specific Targets (initial)

### TPS / Action
- Average TTK (time-to-kill) in equal skill 1v1: 4–8 seconds depending on archetype.
- Hard CC duration usually ≤ 1.5–2.0 s before counter window.
- Hacking channel must be interruptible or punishable.

### Space
- Module diversity > single best loadout.
- Capital / carrier power is strong but requires coordination and is vulnerable to focused strikes.

### Strategy / Map
- Major structures should not flip in under 10–15 minutes of real presence without extreme force.
- Resource nodes remain contestable; no permanent lock without continuous investment.

### MOBA (Aexion Clash)
- Standard MOBA pacing targets (laning → mid → late).
- Faction kits different but win-rate parity goal ±2–3% after tuning.

## 5. Monetization Guardrails

Allowed:
- Cosmetics, visual variants, convenience, exclusive narrative, voice quality, AI tokens.

Forbidden:
- Direct purchase of Contribution / Biomass Rank power.
- Exclusive weapons or abilities that cannot be earned.
- Pay-only matchmaking advantages.

## 6. Tuning Process

1. Data tables are the single source of numerical truth.
2. Changes are recorded in SHARED_AGENT_MEMORY.md.
3. Major shifts require a short design note explaining the goal.
4. Playtest feedback has priority over theory.
