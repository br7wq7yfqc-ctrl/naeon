# NAEON — Weapons, Gear & Equipment

**Version:** 0.1  
**Depends on:** rules/12 crafting, 15 tiers, 04 balance, 19 freemium  
**Reference bar:** skill-based loadouts (Albion-like clarity) + shared tier budgets — **not** pay-stat vertical

---

## 1. Principles

- Gear defines **role and tradeoffs**, not absolute dominance.
- Equivalent power budgets Cybernex ↔ gROT at each tier.
- Shop/sub: **cosmetic skins and convenience only** — no exclusive top-tier DPS blueprints (rules/19).
- Random rolls: cosmetic or tiny QoL bands only; **no** unique permanent DPS from RNG (skill NMS bar).

---

## 2. TPS slots

| Slot | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Main hand | Primary weapon |
| Off hand / focus | Sidearm, shield emitter, biomass focus |
| Body | Integrity / armor curve |
| Systems | Utility implant (scanner, logistics, soft Knowledge UI) |
| Boots / mobility | Move speed soft cap, dash energy |
| Cosmetic | Emission, cloak pattern — zero combat stats |

---

## 3. Weapon families (tier-shared DPS bands)

| Family | Fantasy | Play |
|--------|---------|------|
| Pulse / kinetic | Both | Reliable mid-range |
| Beam / lattice | Cybernex-leaning | Sustained, clean VFX |
| Spore / corrosive | gROT-leaning | DoT pressure, pairs with Infection |
| Heavy launcher | Both | High burst, slow charge |
| Support tool | Both | Repair, cleanse charge, probe — low DPS |

**Tier DPS index (relative, not absolute damage numbers)**  
T0 = 1.0 · T1 = 1.15 · T2 = 1.35 · T3 = 1.55 — apply to **both** faction trees. Exact numbers live in data tables; retune one lever at a time (skill §20).

TTK target with equal skill + equal tier remains ~4.5–6.5 s (rules/04).

---

## 4. Core stats (readable)

| Stat | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Integrity | Effective HP pool |
| Energy capacity / regen | Ability fuel |
| Weapon power | Scales primary damage within tier band |
| Stability | Recoil / bloom resistance |
| Nex Integrity | Soft resist vs Infection duration (not immunity) |
| Biomass affinity | Soft benefit to gROT conversion tools — **not** free DPS |
| Armor / shield curve | Damage type mitigation (kinetic vs corrosive) |

No stat may be sold as a subscription multiplier.

---

## 5. Crafting & acquisition

- T0–T1: quests, vendors, basic craft (rules/15).
- T2–T3: faction trees, alliance projects, long chains.
- Blueprint power items must snap to tier bands; Prompt Studio cannot invent unconstrained DPS.

---

## 6. Durability / risk (soft EVE-like)

Optional repair sink on death in contested space — **not** full loot wipe in safe hubs. Cargo risk separate (logistics).

---

## 7. VS minimum

One T0 Pulse weapon both factions, one body piece, equip UI, no shop power tags.
