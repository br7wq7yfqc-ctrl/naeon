# NAEON — Core Design Pillars

**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Immutable reference for all design and implementation

---

## 1. Asymmetric but Fair Conflict

Cybernex and gROT must feel like different civilizations at war, not reskins.

- Cybernex: protection, harmony, information, Firewall, stable claims, RBE.
- gROT: assimilation, infection, Hacking, swarm pressure, Biomass.

Both must be viable long-term. Counters exist; neither is the only correct faction.

---

## 2. Layered Gameplay, One Universe

Strategy, Space, TPS, and MOBA are layers of the same persistent world.

- Actions in one layer can influence others, but soft caps prevent any single mode from dictating the entire map.
- Arena influence is real but temporary and limited.
- Ownership changes are meaningful and require coordinated effort.

---

## 3. Soft Power over Hard Paywalls

- Knowledge and education give informational and QoL advantages, never raw damage or HP that free players cannot match.
- Subscription and tokens buy convenience, cosmetics, voice, narrative, and generation quota — never combat power or claim strength.
- Premium quests enrich living history; they do not alter MMO balance.

---

## 4. Readable Fantasy

Every major system should be understandable from fiction:

- Hacking feels like subverting machines and implants.
- Firewall feels like defending the Nex.
- Dynamic Ownership looks and plays differently under each flag (Venus harmony vs biomass industry).

If a mechanic cannot be explained in faction fantasy terms, it needs redesign.

---

## 5. Economical Creation

Early development optimizes for cost and speed without sacrificing the dual-theme fantasy:

- Tripo-first generation, free-tier priority.
- One good mesh → many Cybernex/gROT variants in Blender.
- Heavy assets stay out of Git; pipeline feeds neon bucket and local assets/.

---

## 6. Persistent Consequence, Not Endless Steamroll

- Territory and capital assets matter.
- Soft daily/weekly caps, Protected story states, and decay of pure Arena influence keep the map alive for new and returning players.
- Victory should feel earned; defeat should leave room to recover.

---

## 7. AI as Amplifier, Not Authority

- aiNEX / Yandex GPT / Prompt Studio expand content and dialogue.
- Safety filters and design rules still govern what enters the live world.
- Generated educational content supports learning; it does not replace balance authority.

---

## 8. Readable UI

Faction chrome must never hide threat meaning. Hack, Firewall, Infection, and Contested states use shape + text + optional audio, not colour alone. Low-end prioritizes text and threat icons.

---

*All new mechanics and content must be checkable against these pillars.*
