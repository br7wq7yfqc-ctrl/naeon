# NAEON — Economy Rules: RBE (Cybernex) & Biomass (gROT)

**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-06

---

## 1. Design Intent

Two asymmetric but viable economies that express faction fantasy without creating a single dominant strategy.

| Faction | Economy name | Core fantasy |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Cybernex | Contribution / RBE-like | Shared prosperity, knowledge, efficient allocation |
| gROT | Biomass Rank | Growth through assimilation, pressure, conversion |

Neither economy may be solved by real-money purchases.

---

## 2. Cybernex — Contribution Economy

### Sources of Contribution
- Extracting and delivering resources to allied nodes
- Completing faction and alliance quests
- Defending or reclaiming Ownership objects
- Successful educational modules / Knowledge milestones (small bonus)
- Repairing infrastructure and supporting allies

### Sinks
- Constructing and upgrading colony modules
- Crafting advanced components and blueprints
- Maintaining high-tier structures
- Alliance shared projects

### Principles
- Contribution is primarily a measure of useful work for the collective.
- Personal power from Contribution is soft (access, convenience, cosmetic ranks, minor QoL).
- Hard combat stats do not scale directly with total Contribution.

### Early numeric bands (Vertical Slice)
| Action | Contribution gain |
|--------|-------------------|
| Deliver 1 standard resource unit | 1–3 |
| Complete standard quest | 40–80 |
| Participate in successful claim flip | 100–250 |
| Finish educational module (first time) | 15–30 |

---

## 3. gROT — Biomass Economy

### Sources of Biomass Rank progress
- Converting nodes and biomass fields
- Successful Hacking / infection actions
- Destroying or assimilating Cybernex infrastructure
- Swarm objectives and aggressive Ownership pressure
- Feeding carriers and drone networks

### Sinks
- Spawning and sustaining swarm units
- Accelerating infection on Contested objects
- High-tier mutation unlocks (cosmetic + situational tools)
- Maintaining aggressive forward bases

### Principles
- Biomass rewards pressure and risk.
- Temporary power spikes are allowed; permanent runaway is not.
- Infection-related gains are subject to counterplay (Firewall, cleanse, loss of Contested progress).

### Early numeric bands
| Action | Biomass progress |
|--------|------------------|
| Convert small node | 50–120 |
| Land successful Infect Link in PvP | 8–15 |
| Complete aggressive faction quest | 60–100 |
| Hold Contested object to flip | 150–300 |

---

## 4. Shared / Neutral Resources

Physical resources (ore, energy cells, rare components) exist for both factions and feed crafting, modules and logistics.

- Logistics (hauling valuable cargo) is a first-class activity.
- Loss of cargo on destruction is intentional risk.
- Market / exchange between factions is limited or mediated by neutral platforms to preserve asymmetry.

---

## 5. Anti-Runaway Rules

1. Soft caps on daily Contribution / Biomass from repetitive actions.
2. Defending and attacking both remain valuable (no pure turtle or pure steamroll optimum).
3. Ownership flips redistribute economic opportunity.
4. Knowledge Rank never multiplies raw extraction rates beyond a tiny QoL band.

---

## 6. Open Parameters

- Exact conversion ratios resource → Contribution / Biomass
- Crafting tier costs
- Alliance shared pool rules
- Decay or upkeep on high-tier structures
