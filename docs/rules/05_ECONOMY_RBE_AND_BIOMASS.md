# 05 — Economy Rules: RBE / Contribution vs Biomass Rank

**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** Design draft

## 1. Design Goals

- Two distinct economic fantasies that reinforce faction identity.
- No hard pay-to-win conversion between real money and power.
- Clear sinks and sources to keep the economy controllable.
- Support both solo and alliance-scale play.

## 2. Cybernex — Contribution & Resource-Based Economy (RBE)

### Fantasy
Players contribute to the common good. Value is measured by useful work, not hoarded currency.

### Core Resource: Contribution Score
- Earned by: resource delivery, construction, defense, successful quests, educational completion, logistics, alliance tasks.
- Spent / used for: unlocking higher-tier blueprints, structure upgrades, priority access to shared facilities, certain soft benefits.
- Never directly purchased with real money.

### Supporting Resources
- Raw materials (mined / extracted)
- Refined components
- Energy credits (soft internal unit for local exchange, limited player-to-player)

### Key Rules
- Contribution has soft diminishing returns at the highest ranks for pure combat power.
- Alliance-shared Contribution pools exist for large projects.
- RBE facilities can convert excess materials into Contribution or shared stockpiles.

## 3. gROT — Biomass Rank

### Fantasy
Growth of the swarm. Everything feeds the biomass and the will of ROT.

### Core Resource: Biomass Rank / Biomass Points
- Earned by: assimilation of resources, successful infection objectives, destruction or conversion of enemy assets, swarm quests, expansion of controlled territory.
- Spent / used for: evolving forms, unlocking aggressive blueprints, empowering Infection-related abilities, accelerating structure growth.
- Never directly purchased with real money.

### Supporting Resources
- Raw biomass matter
- Corrupted / converted components
- Swarm bandwidth

### Key Rules
- Biomass Rank emphasizes aggressive expansion and conversion.
- High rank gives stronger temporary swarm bonuses but also higher visibility / counter-focus from Cybernex.

## 4. Shared / Neutral Economy Elements

- Logistics (hauling valuable cargo) is profitable and risky for both sides.
- Player-driven contracts and alliance jobs.
- Crafting uses faction-specific recipes that can be reverse-engineered only with significant effort and risk.
- Black market / grey zone trading is possible but risky and monitored by Fair Play systems.

## 5. Monetization Boundaries (Economy)

Allowed:
- Cosmetics, convenience, inventory QoL, narrative packs, AI generation tokens.

Forbidden:
- Direct purchase of Contribution Score or Biomass Rank.
- Buying permanent combat power or exclusive high-tier blueprints that cannot be earned.

## 6. Sinks

- Structure maintenance / upkeep
- Ability and module crafting
- Repair after battles
- Alliance projects
- Temporary boosts that expire
- Failed infection / firewall overuse penalties

## 7. Open Numerical Work

- Exact conversion rates and rank thresholds will live in data tables.
- Initial bands will be set conservatively and tuned after playtests.
