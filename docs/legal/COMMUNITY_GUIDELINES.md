# NAEON — Community Guidelines (Draft)

**Version:** 0.1  
**Companion to:** User Agreement, Fair Play Policy

## Respect
Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing prohibited. IC faction hostility ≠ OOC abuse of players.

## Fair play
No cheats, bots, exploit abuse. Report exploits privately.

## Economy
No RMT of non-cosmetic advantages, accounts, or claim services.

## Chat & voice
No spam, malicious links, or sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance). Alliance leaders moderate voice hubs reasonably.

## UGC & AI
Prompt Studio / aiNEX must not generate illegal content or sexual content with minors.

## Griefing vs competition
Contesting Ownership and fair PvP are gameplay. Login-camp harassment and constructor abuse aimed only at one player are not.

## Enforcement
Warnings → mutes → suspensions → bans. Appeals TBD by operator.

*Draft; final policy needs operator legal review.*
