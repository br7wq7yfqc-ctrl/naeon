# NAEON — Fair Play Policy

**Version:** 0.1 (Draft)  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-05  
**Related documents:** User Agreement, Community Guidelines

The integrity of NAEON depends on fair competition and a healthy community. This Fair Play Policy defines what is allowed and what is not.

---

## 1. Core Principles

- Skill, knowledge, coordination and contribution matter more than money.
- No pay-to-win: real-money purchases must never grant direct, permanent combat or progression power that cannot be obtained through play.
- Soft advantages (cosmetics, convenience, exclusive narrative content, QoL) are permitted.
- Both factions must remain viable and fun.

---

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

### 2.1 Cheating & Technical Abuse

You may **not**:

- Use cheats, hacks, bots, scripts, macros, or any third-party software that gives an unfair advantage.
- Exploit bugs or unintended mechanics for personal or group gain (report them instead).
- Manipulate matchmaking, lag, or network conditions deliberately.
- Use multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions, farm rewards, or boost other accounts ("multi-accounting" for advantage).

### 2.2 Economic Abuse

You may **not**:

- Engage in real-money trading (RMT) of accounts, items, currency, or services.
- Use the game economy for money laundering or any illegal financial activity.
- Artificially inflate or deflate markets through collusion or bots.

### 2.3 Social & Community Abuse

You may **not**:

- Harass, threaten, doxx, or discriminate against other players.
- Use hate speech, extreme toxicity, or sexual content involving minors.
- Impersonate staff, moderators, or official NAEXOS entities.
- Spam, flood chat, or disrupt communication channels.

### 2.4 Faction & Alliance Integrity

- Espionage and in-game betrayal are part of the fantasy when done through legitimate game mechanics.
- Out-of-game coordinated real-world harassment or blackmail is forbidden.

---

## 3. Allowed & Encouraged Behaviour

- Mastering mechanics, Knowledge system, and strategy.
- Forming alliances, diplomacy, and player-driven objectives.
- Using official AI tools (aiNEX, Yandex GPT integrations) within their intended scope.
- Creating content through the in-client prompt tools according to the rules.
- Reporting bugs, cheaters, and policy violations.

---

## 4. Subscription & Monetization Boundaries

| Allowed | Not Allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Cosmetics, skins, visual variants | Direct power increases that cannot be earned |
| Convenience (inventory space, faster travel cosmetics, etc.) | Skipping core progression gates permanently |
| Exclusive narrative / premium quests (non-balance) | Exclusive weapons or abilities that break PvP balance |
| Token packages for AI generation / convenience | Buying Contribution / Biomass Rank directly |
| Voice channels & quality-of-life features | Pay-to-win matchmaking advantages |

---

## 5. Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Warnings
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent ban
- Removal of illegitimately obtained items or progress
- In severe cases, reporting to relevant authorities

We reserve the right to take action based on available evidence, including automated detection systems.

---

## 6. Appeals

Players may appeal enforcement actions through the official support channels. Appeals must be submitted in good faith and within a reasonable time.

---

## 7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Continued use of the Service after updates constitutes acceptance.
