# NAEON — Schism Timeline (Canon Seed)

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Narrative authority  
**Does not grant combat power or unique permanent weapons**

Ages are labeled in **Post-Lattice** convention; absolute years optional for codex UI.

---

## Pre-Schism

| Era | Beat |
|-----|------|
| **Lattice Rise** | Transgalactic NAEXOS network links systems; shared research, resource allocation prototypes (proto-RBE ideals). |
| **Rewrite Debate** | Factions of scientists/engineers argue hard limits on genetic and neural rewriting. Central control vs open mutation. |
| **First Bleed** | Unbounded rewrite packages propagate through the net; early infection signatures appear in unpatched implants. |

---

## The Schism

| Beat | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| **Consensus Break** | No single polity can enforce rewrite limits across the Lattice. |
| **Hibernation Mandate** | Majority of baseline humanity enters long sleep in **NEX** vaults on **ARK** to avoid swarm infection via the net. |
| **Caretaker Divergence** | Anthropomorphic cyber-animal units + remaining **noo-people** (edited humans) adopt protective limits → **Cybernex**. |
| **Swarm Choice** | Those rejecting limits consolidate under **ROT** → **gROT**; biomass doctrine and Hacking as tools of growth. |
| **Network Split** | Firewall doctrine vs Infection doctrine harden; visual and economic languages diverge (rules/09). |

---

## Post-Schism (play era)

| Beat | Play use |
|------|----------|
| **Caretaker Vigil** | Cybernex defends sleepers, runs Contribution economy, studies safe Knowledge. |
| **Vein Expansion** | gROT pushes conversion fronts; Contested systems multiply. |
| **Grey Corridors** | Mirror Expanse and remnant stations mediate fragile trade / information. |
| **Living War** | Ownership flips, Arena soft influence, campaigns Act I–VI (no permanent exclusive DPS paths). |
