## 1. Cybernex — life under the Lattice

### NEX / ARK

- **Sleepers** rest in vault districts; public ritual is maintenance, monitoring, and “wake readiness” drills — not tourist spectacle.
- **Contribution boards** display shared needs (Venus-Project-like allocation aesthetic): energy, organics, repair cycles.
- **Gardens in chrome:** living plant systems intertwined with clean neon architecture (Ownership visual when Cybernex holds a node).
- **Knowledge halls:** optional educational modules; soft Subject Mastery (rules/08).
- **Social tone:** duty, clarity, care for the sleeping; humour is dry, technical.

### Daily loops (fiction)

Shift rotations on perimeter → extract/logistics → archive study → rest in habitat modules. Alliance hubs add constructor briefings (rules/11).

## 2. gROT — life in the Vein

### ROT-Prime / Spire culture

- **Hierarchy is visible:** ornament and address forms mark Pack → Fang → Spine → Voice.
- **Conversion as meaning:** matter that is not yet swarm is “unfinished.”
- **Industrial biomass:** vats, spore yards, ash-lit forges; beauty is density and growth, not garden harmony.
- **Voice of ROT:** broadcasts, ritual echo-priests (rules/07 GR_VOICE_OF_ROT); loyalty beats in campaign, not mandatory paywall power.
- **Social tone:** hungry efficiency, dark humour, contempt for “limits.”

### Daily loops (fiction)

Hunt/raid marks → harvest → conversion → hierarchy tests → rest in clustered dens.
