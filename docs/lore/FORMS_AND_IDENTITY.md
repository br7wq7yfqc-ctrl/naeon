# NAEON — Forms, Species Identity & Presentation

**Version:** 0.1  
**Constraint:** Form is **identity and animation language** within **shared balance budgets** (skill §3). No form-exclusive permanent DPS.

---

## 1. Cybernex forms

Caretaker chassis: anthropomorphic animal-derived cyborg frames, edited against easy remote Infection.

| Form family | Fantasy | Soft gameplay flavour (not power) |
|-------------|---------|-------------------------------------|
| **Canine** | Guardians, wardens | Stance animations, bark/alert VO |
| **Feline** | Scouts, quiet ops | Idle grace, soft footfall SFX |
| **Avian** | Pathfinders, messengers | Head motion, wing-cloak cosmetics |
| **Marine mammal** | Logistics, deep-dock culture | Heavy stable gait, water-adjacent hubs |
| **Other approved** | Diversity within art bible | Same slot budgets |

**Noo-people:** remaining humans with cybernetic/genetic limits; same ability slots and tier gear as animal forms. Cultural role often Archivist / policy / NEX liaison.

Activity limits from protective editing are **fiction and soft QoL constraints**, compensated by Contribution economy and social tools — never a hard “this form cannot equip T3”.

## 3. Player choice

- Faction choice at start (or rare narrative transfer later — design gated).
- Form/skin selectable within faction art set.
- Loadout and ranks follow rules/16, 21 — form does not replace gear progression.
