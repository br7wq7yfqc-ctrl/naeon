# NAEON — Neutrals, Remnants & Grey Actors

**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Third-tone content without a third full playable faction (yet)  
**Future:** other playable races possible later — do not hard-lock systems against it

---

## 1. Mirror Expanse mediators

- Station clusters that refuse exclusive Cybernex or gROT flags.
- Services: limited trade, information brokers, repair at premium risk rates.
- Diplomacy: can host truce talks (flavour for rules/23); cannot override war timers.

---

## 2. Pre-Schism remnants

- Automated caretaker AIs with incomplete mandates.
- Ruined Lattice hubs (Echo Ruins) with archive cores (RELICS_AND_PHENOMENA).
- “Still-sleeping” secondary vault myths — story only until design opens a vault act.

---

## 3. Independent packs / free captains

- Small NPC fleets and surface gangs for PvE pressure.
- May lean aesthetic CX or gROT without alliance membership.
- Loot snaps to tier tables; no unique endgame monopoly items.

---

## 4. Design rules

- Neutrals enrich sandbox and diplomacy theatre.
- They must not become the only efficient power path (no “grey P2W”).
- If a future playable race is added, map it through asymmetry + shared budgets like CX/gROT.
